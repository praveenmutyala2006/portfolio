My Life Through Places

My journey hasn't been about brief holidays, but about living and growing in diverse cities. From my birth in Delhi to my student life in Kerala, each place has shaped who I am. This blog chronicles the landscapes, cultures, and memories that define my story, creating a collection of cherished moments that were not just visited, but truly lived.

Delhi

My journey began here, in the heart of the capital. Delhi is where I took my first breath, a place of immense history and heritage. Though I was too small to grasp the grandeur of its monuments or the depth of its culture, it remains the starting point of my existence—a vague but significant memory of where it all started.

Arunachal Pradesh

Arunachal was a world defined by nature’s raw beauty. I vividly remember the waterfalls and streams, and a culture so unique it felt like a different world. My fondest memories are of playing with small marbles and rubber balls, watching them rush away with the flow of the river. I still recall the winding, circling climb up the hills—even though I was so small, those sights and the feeling of the mountains are etched in my mind as some of the nicest memories I possess.

Chennai

Moving to Chennai was a shift to a completely different environment. Suddenly, I was surrounded by beaches that brought a sense of incredible calm. This was the age where I truly started to become aware of the world around me. Life was stress-free, filled with good friends and the pure joy of childhood days spent under the sun. It was a time of awakening, where the sea breeze seemed to carry away any worry.

Hyderabad – Kanha Shanti Vanam

Moving to Hyderabad was more than a relocation; it was a homecoming to Kanha Shanti Vanam. This is the place I truly feel is home—a sanctuary that nurtured my soul. It blossomed into one of the happiest chapters of my life. Amidst the pristine surroundings, I lived my "best teenage years", defined by school fun, intense sports, and lifelong friendships. It was here that I found my footing, studied hard, and grew immense confidence. Kanha is where my heart is, and it has shaped much of who I am.

Kerala

Then came the transition to Kerala, securing a seat for B.Tech in Artificial Intelligence at Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham. It was unexpected, but the excitement of living in this serene place, right next to the beach, was overwhelming. It brought back a wave of Chennai nostalgia but with its own unique charm. I simply love it here—the peace, the vibe, and the journey of becoming an engineer in such a beautiful setting.
